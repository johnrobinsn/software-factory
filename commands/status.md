Invoke the `status-report` skill.

Produces one choreographed status covering: in-flight worktrees, shipped-since-last-status, needs-your-judgment, blocked, recent checkpoints, backlog top.

No arguments. If the operator wants a scoped status (e.g., "just the worktrees" or "just what needs my judgment"), they can ask in prose after the initial status — this command always produces the full summary.
